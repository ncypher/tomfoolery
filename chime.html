02 / Carry the signal, keep the detail

The photograph stays.
Its fingerprint travels.

Radio airtime is shared. Chime’s photo-attestation design keeps the original file on the device and sends a compact hash separately.

An interactive analogy

Change a detail.
Change the fingerprint.

This tiny example hashes text so you can see the principle. In Chime, the fingerprint is calculated from the exact bytes of the selected photograph.

SHA-256 / computed on your deviceCalculating…

This example does not select, store, or upload a photograph.

What the record can say

A later file can be compared with the fingerprint that was recorded. If its bytes change, the fingerprint changes.

What it cannot establish

A hash alone does not prove that a scene is real, who took the photo, or when it was taken. Co-signing adds a neighbor’s claim; it does not authenticate a person.

Shared attention is valuable. Being precise about what the evidence means is part of protecting it.
