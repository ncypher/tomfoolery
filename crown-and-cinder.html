How to play

Click a province to inspect it. Your teal provinces accept building and recruitment orders. Select a neighboring rival province, then press Invade. Each season rivals grow, raid, trade, or attack according to their temperament.

Keyboard: E = end season · F = farm · R = recruit · A = invade
