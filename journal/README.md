# Living Code Journal

A place for small observations that may want to become software.

The journal is not a demand to publish on a schedule. It is an invitation to wonder. An entry can remain a paragraph, become a sketch, or grow into a self-contained interactive artifact.

## Invitations

- What surprised me this week?
- What did an AI help me notice that I had not considered?
- What idea refused to leave me alone?
- What system did I see differently after thinking in feedback loops?
- What would make a beautiful artifact?
- What could be understood by moving a slider instead of reading a paragraph?

## A small entry

Each entry can contain three parts:

### A thought

A few honest lines. Free writing, dictated fragments, questions, or an observation.

### A sketch

An SVG, diagram, animation, interface fragment, pseudocode, or description of motion.

### An artifact

A focused piece of software. Roughly 150 lines is a useful invitation, not a rule: one idea, self-contained where practical, and allowed to remain imperfect.

## Naming

Use a date and a short phrase:

```text
journal/2026/08-01-the-space-between.md
journal/2026/08-07-orchestra.md
journal/2026/08-16-feedback-loops.md
```

When an entry becomes interactive, place the implementation under `experiments/` and link the two together.

> Do not make yourself write. Make yourself wonder.
