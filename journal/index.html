Room 04 · Living Code Journal

Do not make yourself write. Make yourself wonder.

This is a place for observations that may want to become software. An entry can remain a paragraph, become a sketch, grow into an interactive artifact, or change its author’s mind and stop there.

The journal is not a production schedule. It is an invitation to notice.

Invitations

What surprised me this week?
What idea refused to leave me alone?
What did an AI help me notice that I had not considered?
What system looked different after I noticed its feedback?
What could be understood by moving a slider instead of reading a paragraph?
What thought would make a beautiful artifact?
