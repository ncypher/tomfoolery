RECEIVER GAME

Can you hear like the radio?

Hide the answer, inspect the chirp, and use the symbol slider to match the mystery symbol.

What to notice: LoRa's symbol is represented as a cyclic shift of a chirp across the configured bandwidth. At SF7 there are 27 = 128 symbol values; at SF12 there are 4096. With 125 kHz bandwidth, increasing SF also increases symbol duration from about 1.024 ms at SF7 to 32.768 ms at SF12. Longer symbols are part of the tradeoff behind LoRa's remarkable sensitivity: lower data rate and longer airtime in exchange for more processing gain.
