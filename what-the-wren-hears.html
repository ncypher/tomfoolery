RECEIVER GAME

Can you hear like the radio?

Hide the answer, inspect the chirp, and use the symbol slider to match the mystery symbol.

What to notice: LoRa uses chirp spread spectrum: frequency sweeps across the configured channel bandwidth while information is represented through cyclic shifts of that chirp. At SF7 there are 27 = 128 symbol values; at SF12 there are 4096. With 125 kHz bandwidth, symbol duration grows from about 1.024 ms at SF7 to 32.768 ms at SF12. The longer symbol time trades data rate and airtime for greater processing gain and sensitivity.
